Invocation of the gods in the Moon
-

A Manichaean text in Parthian.

Eternal praise from the whole community that has been pardoned.

Chief of the messengers, Lord, Friend, Jesus, Savior, Ruler of the

holy religion -

You are eternally holy!

The first of those that go out ... who dispel the dark foes,

beloved of the Lights -

You are eternally holy!

Merciful Mother, Maiden of Light, soul of the God Zurvan,

head of all wisdom, who has enlightened all the gods -

You are eternally holy!

Soul gathering angels, seven shiplords ...

...
