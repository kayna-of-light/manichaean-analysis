The Hymn on Body-and-Soul -

A Manichaean liturgical hymn in Parthian.

Sweet place of rest, oh garden!

May you be a sweet place of rest for me

Return to me, remain in me.

May we be of one accord through your beneficence.

Here ends the hymn on Body and Soul.
