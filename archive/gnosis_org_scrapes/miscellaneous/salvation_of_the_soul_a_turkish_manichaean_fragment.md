## Salvation of the Soul.

Salvation of the Soul.

Like the gray wolf I will walk with you;

like the black raven I will stay on earth.

Like charcoal to the disease,

like the spittle to the whetstone I will be.

You are our powerful and great ruler.

Like gold rounded,

like a ball rounded,

you are our glorious wise lord.

And your numerous people

at your wide breast,

at your long seam,

you keep and proect, you nurse, take care of.
MANICHAEAN WRITINGS
GNOSIS ARCHIVE
GNOSTIC SOCIETY
LIBRARY
