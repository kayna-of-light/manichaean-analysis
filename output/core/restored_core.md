*and explicit Christian overlay — have been removed. Mixed passages*
*have been repaired to recover the teaching content. The text is*
*presented in its original chapter order.*

## Chapter 0

*This introduction is largely justificatory/editorial: Mani’s self-presentation, injunctions to record oral teaching, and comparison with earlier apostles dominate (PASTORAL/FRAME/OVERLAY). **Temporal observations:**
- ¶1: Cosmogonic exposition about primordial separation and aeons, without citation or institutional exhortation; impersonal ‘beginning’ teaching register despite lacunae.
- ¶2: Continues primordial two-principles teaching (light/darkness) in expository mode; no frame/citation markers.
- ¶3: Core cosmogony (assault of Darkness) embedded in a later instructional wrapper about ‘first lesson/eternality’ and written materials.
- ¶4: Classic Manichaean cosmogonic sequence: emanation/descent of the First Man and armour of light; systematic mythic mechanism, no citation.
- ¶5: Systematic cosmological construction by the Living Spirit; process/structure voice (‘set in order’, ‘constructed’, ‘foundation’).
- ¶6: Numbered/structural mapping elements (‘three earths’, ‘four mountains’, ‘three vessels’) and cosmos-as-bond mechanism are strong indicators of older systematic teaching.
- ¶7: Named cosmic redeemer (Third Ambassador) in the cosmogonic sequence; not a Gospel-derived ‘Saviour’ citation.
- ¶8: Eschatological consummation in mythic-process terms (cosmic fire, purification/ascent, binding of darkness, final monarchy of the Father). No NT citation formula.
- ¶9: Brief doctrinal axiom about primordial mixture; fits the core cosmogonic discourse though very fragmentary.
- ¶10: Authorizing/bibliographic self-reference to Mani’s canon (‘I have written them in my books of light: The Great Gospel… Epistles… Psalms…’). This is not the older impersonal cosmological layer but a later editorial/self-legitimating frame for the compilation.
- ¶11: Apologetic criterion for ‘writer/teacher of truth’ plus first-person self-assertion (‘I also… I have revealed… I have given…’): legitimating rhetoric rather than cosmological mechanism.
- ¶12: Underlying schematic/numbered correspondential structure (twelve…, doors, four corners) looks like older systematic teaching, but the paragraph is heavily lacunose and has been blended with later first-person exhortation.
- ¶13: Institutional/community instruction about recording homilies and lessons; explicit ‘elect and catechumens’ marks later church operational layer.
- ¶14: Direct admonition to remember/write teachings; hortatory second-person voice, not cosmological exposition.
- ¶15: Pastoral distribution by audience categories (‘elect’, ‘suckling babes’, polemic against sects/heresies) and self-testimony; not the impersonal core register.
- ¶16: Too fragmentary to extract a secure core segment; the surviving language is largely about apostles/fathers, scriptures, church, and books—i.e., later legitimating/Christianizing discourse rather than systematic cosmology.
- ¶17: Explicit Jesus-on-earth narrative (‘Jesus trod… land of the west… his parables… signs and wonders… wrote a book’): enters via Christian/Gospel tradition, hence overlay.
- ¶18: Comparative apostolic biography about Zarathustra not writing books; serves the justificatory frame for Mani’s own writings rather than presenting cosmological mechanics.
- ¶19: Parallel justificatory anecdote about Buddha and disciples writing later; functions as editorial argument for textual preservation.
- ¶20: General institutional warning about church decay and adulteration of teaching if not written; hortatory and community-facing.
- ¶21: Commandments/scriptures and ‘write after me so that it leads you not astray’—operational instruction; mixed with church vocabulary.
- ¶22: Continues exhortation to remember and write portions of oral teaching; not cosmological.
- ¶23: Closing encouragement about the benefit/enlightening effect of writing; epilogic framing rather than core teaching.
